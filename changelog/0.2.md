# Changelog: 0.2

## [Unreleased Changes]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-16

### Changed
- The Nether has no roof: the bedrock roof and the rock cap over the caverns are gone, and the Nether is open above.
- Its upper part is reshaped for arriving from above: highlands and mesas up to about Y 120 with overhanging rims, cut by wide basins down to the lava sea. The caverns stay inside the highlands, open to the sky in places.
- The Nether's logical height is 256 (was 128), so Immersive Portals: Aeronautics' dimension stack puts the Nether's ceiling at Y 256, high above the terrain, and nether portals may be placed up to there.
- The Nether's cave carvers start only below Y 64, so they no longer cut trenches into the open tops.

### Added
- Crimson and warped forests grow in groves and glades: dense stands of huge fungi alternate with open clearings, instead of the same density everywhere.
- Giant crimson and warped fungi, 24 to 40 blocks tall, stand in their forests now and then, some rising out of lava basins. The middle of their caps is flat enough to land an airship on.

### Compatibility
- Open Nether refuses to load with Incendium or Amplified Nether, which replace the Nether's terrain too, and warns about Jaden's Nether Expansion, whose default config does the same (its message says which settings to change).
